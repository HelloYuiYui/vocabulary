# DELF checklist: A1 to B2

Themes to cover for each level. Tick a theme once you can understand it, talk about it for a minute or two, and write a short text on it without looking things up.

## A1: Survival basics

- [ ] **Se présenter**: name, age, nationality, job, languages spoken.
- [ ] **Famille et entourage**: describing family members and basic relationships.
- [ ] **Chiffres, dates, heure**: telling the time, dates, prices, phone numbers.
- [ ] **Description simple**: basic physical appearance and personality adjectives.
- [ ] **Chez soi**: rooms, furniture, and saying where you live.
- [ ] **Routine quotidienne**: daily actions, mostly with reflexive verbs.
- [ ] **Nourriture et courses**: buying food, quantities, ordering in a café.
- [ ] **La ville**: naming places in town and asking where something is.
- [ ] **Transports**: means of transport and buying a ticket.
- [ ] **Météo et saisons**: describing the weather and seasons.
- [ ] **Goûts et loisirs**: saying what you like, dislike and do in your free time.
- [ ] **Vêtements**: clothes, colours, sizes, shopping for them.
- [ ] **Le corps**: body parts and saying « j'ai mal à… ».
- [ ] **Écrits courts**: filling in a form, writing a postcard or a short message.

## A2: Everyday independence

- [ ] **Raconter au passé**: narrating events with passé composé and imparfait.
- [ ] **Santé**: describing symptoms at the doctor's or pharmacy and understanding advice.
- [ ] **Logement**: reading a rental ad, describing a flat, contacting a landlord.
- [ ] **Itinéraires**: giving and following directions around a town.
- [ ] **Voyages**: booking a hotel, describing a trip, handling problems on holiday.
- [ ] **Restaurant**: booking, ordering, and making a polite complaint.
- [ ] **Achats et services**: bank, post office, returning an item, asking for a refund.
- [ ] **Travail et études**: describing your job, studies and work schedule.
- [ ] **Projets**: talking about future plans with futur proche and futur simple.
- [ ] **Comparer**: comparing places, objects and people.
- [ ] **Sorties et invitations**: proposing, accepting, refusing and arranging to meet.
- [ ] **Fêtes et traditions**: describing celebrations and customs.
- [ ] **Téléphone et emails**: leaving messages and writing simple formal emails.
- [ ] **Écrits personnels**: thank-you notes, apologies, invitations, short personal letters.

## B1: Opinions and abstract topics

- [ ] **Donner son avis**: expressing, justifying and nuancing an opinion, agreeing or disagreeing.
- [ ] **Expériences et souvenirs**: longer narratives including the plus-que-parfait.
- [ ] **Sentiments**: expressing emotions, often triggering the subjunctive.
- [ ] **Hypothèses et conseils**: conditionnel and si clauses for advice, wishes and suggestions.
- [ ] **Actualités et médias**: understanding and summarising news items.
- [ ] **Monde du travail**: job interviews, working conditions, workplace conflicts.
- [ ] **Éducation**: studying, training and education systems.
- [ ] **Environnement**: ecology, sustainable habits, climate issues.
- [ ] **Consommation**: shopping habits and writing a formal complaint.
- [ ] **Technologie**: social media, screens, digital life pros and cons.
- [ ] **Santé et bien-être**: lifestyle, sport, stress, healthy habits.
- [ ] **Culture**: reviewing a film, book or exhibition.
- [ ] **Vie en société**: volunteering, solidarity, city versus countryside, neighbours.
- [ ] **Interculturel**: cultural differences and living abroad.
- [ ] **Écrits argumentés**: opinion essays, forum posts and formal letters of around 160 words.

## B2: Argumentation and nuance

- [ ] **Argumenter**: building a structured case with connectors like *néanmoins*, *en revanche*, *d'autant plus que*.
- [ ] **Débattre**: defending a position, conceding points and refuting counterarguments live.
- [ ] **Société et inégalités**: discrimination, gender equality, poverty, social mobility.
- [ ] **Politique et citoyenneté**: elections, institutions, civic engagement, protest.
- [ ] **Économie et travail**: remote work, unemployment, globalisation, the future of work.
- [ ] **Sciences et éthique**: medical progress, AI, bioethics and their dilemmas.
- [ ] **Médias et information**: fake news, media trust, freedom of the press.
- [ ] **Environnement en profondeur**: climate policy, energy transition, individual versus collective responsibility.
- [ ] **Éducation et société**: school reform, inequality in education, lifelong learning.
- [ ] **Santé publique**: healthcare systems, prevention, mental health awareness.
- [ ] **Langue et identité**: multilingualism, francophonie, immigration and integration.
- [ ] **Arts et patrimoine**: the role of culture, cultural funding, heritage preservation.
- [ ] **Urbanisme et mode de vie**: housing crisis, mobility, gentrification, consumption trends.
- [ ] **Nuancer et reformuler**: hedging, rephrasing, expressing degrees of certainty.
- [ ] **Registres de langue**: switching between formal, standard and familiar French appropriately.
- [ ] **Écrits argumentatifs**: formal letters, articles and essays of around 250 words.
